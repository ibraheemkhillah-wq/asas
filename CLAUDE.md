# CALL & RENT design rules

- Every design is delivered at the highest resolution: posts 2160x2700, stories 2160x3840, Canva exports `pro` at width 2160 or more. Never send a 1080 version.
- Images come from Canva only, never Higgsfield.
- Deliver finished designs as file attachments (for WhatsApp: send as Document).
- Text spacing must be right on the first delivery (recurring feedback). Noto Kufi Arabic inks far beyond its size: about 1.0x the size above the baseline and 0.3-0.5x below it. Place each line from measured `textbbox` extents, never from the font size alone, and keep at least 24px (1080 units) clear between any two lines, between text and underlines, chips or icons, and between text and busy parts of the photo.
- Before sending, check every slide at full size for touching or overlapping text, lines cutting through letters, and labels sitting on drawings.
