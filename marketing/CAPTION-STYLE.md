# CALL & RENT: social caption style (always use this)

Every reel or post caption follows this pattern. Deliver it inside a single copyable text box (code block). Write in standard Arabic (فصحى). Use exactly 5 hashtags.

## Rules
- **Title line:** a short hook with stretched letters (tatweel), for example «حــادث واحـد… وفـاتـورة لا تـنـتـهـي», followed by 1–2 emojis.
- **🔵 problem paragraph:** the situation or pain point in 1–2 sentences.
- **🔵 «مع … من CALL & RENT:»** followed by three benefits marked 🟦, 🟧 and 🟥.
- **🔵 «كيف تحصل عليه/عليها؟»:** how to get it.
  - Optional services (kasko, child seat, delivery and so on) carry extra fees and are subject to terms.
  - Never claim coverage, prices or "free" without verification.
- **💬 engagement question:** ends with «شاركنا … في التعليقات 👇».
- **Separators:** three lines containing only `.`, then the fixed footer, then three `.` lines again, then the hashtags.
- **Hashtags:** start with `#من_خدماتنا` when the post is about a service. Then the topic hashtag, `#تأجير_سيارات_اسطنبول`, `#CallRent`, and `#اسطنبول`.
